# 🔥 Burning Fireplace: Cozy Crackling Logs & Soothing Glow (4K UHD)

A luxury interactive 4K UHD real fireplace relaxation & sleep application built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**. Designed for deep sleep, meditation, study focus, cozy ambiance, and bedroom illumination.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🪵 100% Real Live-Action 4K Fireplace Footage
- **No cartoon animations**: Pure, high-definition live-action 4K video of real burning hardwood logs on an iron grate with glowing coals underneath.
- **6 Curated Fireplace Atmospheres**:
  1. **Cozy Crackling Logs 4K (Classic)**: The iconic stone hearth with split hardwood logs on an iron grate and deep embers.
  2. **4K Crackling Hearth & Deep Embers**: 3-hour real burning logs for deep relaxation and stress relief.
  3. **Winter Cabin Blizzard & Roaring Fire**: Intense glowing hearth with a howling snow blizzard outside.
  4. **Gentle Sleep Fireplace & Soft Coals**: Low, rhythmic soothing coals designed for falling asleep.
  5. **Warm Golden Hearth 4K**: Luminous amber flames dancing in a stone firebox.
  6. **Rustic Forest Campfire Under the Stars**: Real pine wood campfire in the woods.
- **Custom Video URL Support**: Load any real fireplace YouTube URL or direct `.mp4` video with one click.

### 2. 🔊 100% Authentic Real Sound (Zero Artificial Buzzing)
- **Authentic Recorded Audio**: Plays the pure binaural recorded crackles, wood pops, and furnace draft from the 4K footage.
- **Real Nature Audio Layers**:
  - 🌧️ **Gentle Rain**: Real rainfall against the window (`rain.mp3`).
  - 🌙 **Night Ambiance**: Authentic nighttime woods & gentle crickets (`night.mp3`).
  - 🐦 **Forest Birds**: Real morning countryside birdsong (`birds.mp3`).
- **Zero Synthetic Noise**: All artificial oscillators and buzzing crickets have been completely eliminated.
- **Master & Individual Faders**: Mix the fire, rain, night, and bird sounds to your personal preference.

### 3. 💡 Soothing Room Glow & Lighting Controls
- **Ambient Room Illumination**: Dynamic warm lighting that projects onto the viewer's room and walls.
- **Warmth Palette**: Choose between Warm Amber, Candlelight Gold, Deep Crimson, Honey Hearth, Midnight Violet, Warm Peach, or pick any custom HEX color.
- **Cinematic Edge Vignette**: Dims the edges to create a cozy, dark bedroom theater atmosphere.
- **Optical Tuning**: Quick sliders for Warmth, Brightness, and Contrast.

### 4. 🌙 Sleep & Relaxation Features
- **Sleep Timer**: Preset duration pills (15m, 30m, 45m, 1h, 1.5h, 2h) with a **gentle 5-minute sound fade-out** and **nightlight auto-dimming**.
- **4-7-8 Breathing Guide**: Optional glowing sleep breathing circle to synchronize heart rate for sleep.
- **Minimalist Bedroom Clock**: Ambient time display with relaxing mantras.

### 5. 🖥️ Ultra-Clean Minimalist UI
- **Zen Floating Pill Dock**: Compact, frosted-glass floating dock that auto-hides after 3.5 seconds of inactivity.
- **Click Outside to Close**: Modals dismiss immediately when clicking anywhere on the darkened backdrop.
- **Double-Click for Fullscreen**: Double-click or double-tap anywhere on screen to toggle true 4K borderless fullscreen.

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|:---:|:---|
| <kbd>F</kbd> | Toggle 4K Fullscreen |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Play / Pause Sound |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Mute / Unmute All Audio |
| <kbd>H</kbd> | Hide / Reveal Controls HUD |
| <kbd>B</kbd> | 4-7-8 Sleep Breathing Exercise |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | Toggle Bedroom Clock & Quotes |
| <kbd>1</kbd> – <kbd>6</kbd> | Quick Fireplace Switch |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close Any Open Dialog |
